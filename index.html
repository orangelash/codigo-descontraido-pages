Before you ask

The questions everybody asks

You're a small company. What if you're unavailable?

The scope and the written record exist so the work can be picked up by someone else. Where a job needs a specialist, they are named up front. And we do not take on more than we can hold — which is the other half of saying no.

Can you work under NDA?

Yes, and it is the norm. Client names and engagement detail are never published — which is precisely why there is no logo wall on this page.

Can we see references?

Arranged directly, where a client agrees to it. Never published, and never used as marketing without asking first.

Do you know our stack?

Nine years across backend services, data platforms, industrial IoT and blockchain, in Python-centred and container-native environments. Where we do not know it, you hear that on the first call rather than in month two.

Are you actually an independent business?

A company since 2022, with its own premises, staff and business expenses, its own insurance, and every engagement contracted business to business. Invoicing, VAT and compliance are handled at our end.

Which language do we work in?

Portuguese, English or Spanish — spoken and written. Deliverables in whichever of those you need them.
