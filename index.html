The record is the deliverable

We say no

Before you ask

The questions everybody asks

How are engagements staffed?

To the work. Where a job needs more hands or a particular specialism, we bring in the people for it — named, and contracted under the same agreement as the rest, so you deal with one party throughout. The written scope and record exist so that the work never depends on any single person's memory.

Can you work under NDA?

Yes, and it is the norm. Client names and engagement detail are never published — which is precisely why there is no logo wall on this page.

Can we see references?

Arranged directly, where a client agrees to it. Never published, and never used as marketing without asking first.

Do you know our stack?

Nine years across backend services, data platforms, industrial IoT and blockchain, in Python-centred and container-native environments. Where we do not know it, we say so and scope around it.

Are you actually an independent business?

Yes — a company since 2022, not a person invoicing through one. Its own premises, equipment and business expenses, its own staff, its own insurance, VAT registered, and every engagement contracted business to business. What that means for you: invoicing, VAT and compliance are handled at our end, and you contract with an entity that carries its own liability.

What happens to our code and our findings?

Under NDA as standard. Source, credentials and findings stay on encrypted company equipment, are never shared with anyone not named in the engagement, and are deleted at the end of it on request. Nothing is used as marketing, in any form, without asking first.

What does it cost?

Retained and embedded work runs to a three-month minimum — below that the ramp-up costs you more than it returns. Fixed-scope assessments and the two-week delivery diagnosis are priced on their own and are not held to it, and training is quoted per programme. Rates are given on the first call, once there is enough scope to price honestly; since that call is free, you have the number before you commit to anything. Nothing runs open-ended without a cap you agreed to.

Which language do we work in?

Portuguese, English or Spanish — spoken and written. Deliverables in whichever of those you need them.
