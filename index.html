Operating principles

How we work

Independent by design

An established company with its own staff, premises and business expenses. Engagements are contracted business to business.

Direct principals

You work with the people doing the work. Specialists are brought in where a job needs them, named up front.

Remote-first

Portugal-based and set up for clients across the EU and internationally. On site where a job genuinely needs it.

Flexible engagement

Fixed-scope assessment, advisory retainer, embedded delivery, or a single workshop. Scoped before it starts.

Documented by default

Findings, decisions and handover material are written down. The record is part of the deliverable.

Confidentiality respected

Client names and engagement detail are not published. References are arranged directly where a client agrees to it.
